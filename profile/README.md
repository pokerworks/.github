PokerWorks is rebuilding social, play-money poker from first principles: real-time gameplay, transparent fairness, and respect for the depth of the game.

Our public GitHub space is for community resources, open technical references, and selected tooling around poker software, research, game theory, fairness, and operational reliability.

## Featured

- [Awesome Poker](https://github.com/pokerworks/awesome-poker) - A curated list of poker software, research, tools, datasets, and learning resources.

## Focus Areas

- Poker engines, evaluators, solvers, and simulation tooling.
- Hand histories, datasets, formats, and developer workflows.
- Fair play, security, observability, and resilient platform operations.
- Research and open resources for builders, researchers, operators, clubs, communities, and serious students of the game.
